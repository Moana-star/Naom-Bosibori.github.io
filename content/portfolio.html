04 / Approach

Good work starts with the right questions.

A practical, evidence-led process keeps technical content useful, accurate and aligned with the people who need it.

01

UNDERSTAND

Clarify the goal

Define the audience, the question they need answered, the deliverable and what success should mean for this specific task.

02

RESEARCH

Check the evidence

Review relevant sources, technical context and assumptions. Separate verified facts from open questions that need validation.

03

STRUCTURE

Make it clear

Organise information around reader needs, use descriptive headings and make the next step easy to find.

04

VALIDATE

Review before delivery

Check links, claims, terminology, accessibility and the agreed scope. Flag limitations rather than hiding uncertainty.

Working principle
Clear scope. Traceable evidence. Honest communication about what is complete and what still needs work.
