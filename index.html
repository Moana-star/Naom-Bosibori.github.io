01 / PAYMENTS
Sandbox prototype
FEATURED CASE STUDY

PayProof AI

Evidence first. AI explains the record. Verified payment data decides.

An evidence-led payment operations prototype for small online sellers. It turns customer order messages into reviewable order drafts, supports PayPal Sandbox checkout and uses server-side provider verification to establish payment status. AI helps organise and explain recorded information; it does not decide whether a payment has been received.

The problem

Small sellers need a clearer way to turn order messages into structured records and review payment evidence without relying on screenshots or assumptions.

What I built

AI-assisted order intake, catalogue-based total validation, Sandbox checkout and verification, payment evidence and a seller-facing review workflow.

Key engineering decision

Payment status is determined by server-side provider verification—not a browser redirect, screenshot or AI response.

Current status: hackathon prototype with a public preview. PayPal Sandbox only—not a live-money processor, bank feed, fraud-detection service or production accounting system.

What stood out in the review

Krishna highlighted the portfolio’s clear structure and its coverage of technical writing, SEO, documentation and AI-related topics. He specifically noted the reader-friendly explanations of technical SEO, APIs, RAG and search, along with the practical checklists, workflows, examples and references.
